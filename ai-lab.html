Important limitation: the detector uses a general COCO model and the classifier uses general ImageNet categories. They are not trained to recognise Singaporean dishes such as laksa, chicken rice or nasi lemak, nor to identify every ingredient. We will replace these baselines with NutriGO-trained models after collecting and evaluating labelled images. Do not use predictions for nutrition or health decisions.
